Extension messaging

Load messaging-a.safariextension and messaging-b.safariextension from ~/Desktop/AquaWebKit-Extensions in Extension Builder. Results: window.__results.

  • ?case=disabled: with messaging-b disabled, messages and ports to it fail.
  • ?case=bfcache: a content script's port and its unanswered messages close when its page enters the back/forward cache, with Chrome's error on the other side, while the page itself is told nothing; the page goes to ?case=away and back by itself.
  • ?case=tabpage: messaging-b opens its page.html in a background tab; once __results.phase is "reload-or-disable-messaging-b", Reload messaging-b in Extension Builder or disable it; the page stops receiving messages, its port's other end disconnects, and in the page's tab window.__pageState records the browser.* calls throwing.
  • ?case=restoredpage: with a page.html of messaging-b that Safari restored with the session in another tab, the page answers messaging-a's messages once Safari loads messaging-b.
  • ?case=reload: once __results.phase is "reload-messaging-a", click Reload for messaging-a in Extension Builder; the port its old content script holds disconnects on the other side, the old content script's messaging calls throw "Extension context invalidated.", and a port the reloaded version opens from this document works.
